# Publication review and claim corrections

This review supersedes stronger language in earlier status notes, paper/video
drafts and conversation. Historical experimental receipts remain unchanged.

- No viable final NEW-crossing improvement or superiority is established.
- Satellite companies are prospective users, not an evaluated competitor set.
- 10,000 fault trials repeatedly sample a finite set of hand-authored mutations.
  V1 has nine cases; V3 samples 299 distinct combinations of twelve mutations.
  These are software regression checks, not 10,000 independent operational
  observations, a statistical safety guarantee, or research novelty evidence.
- The unguarded serializer comparison is a deliberately minimal reference.
  It cannot establish a substantial improvement over actual SEP systems.
  Zero errors should be reported as test-suite results, not a breakthrough.
- The original compact model's nonfinite outputs are reproducible, but their
  root cause has not been isolated by a controlled replay. A prior statement
  attributing the first failure to changing helper code was unsupported. An
  additional attribution to feature overflow remains a hypothesis, even though
  the signed-log arm ran without that failure. Inspect all-missing behavior,
  per-feature support, scaling and layer outputs before claiming causality.
- The admission V2 policy is supplied separately from the verified receipt;
  the new era/revision/magnitude policy is not yet cryptographically bound.
  Arrays are caller supplied and not linked to a verified inference bundle.
  This is an offline synthetic prototype, not a deployed safety boundary.
- The paper is a draft and the video is a narrative, not a produced video.
  The current IRIS site specifies a synopsis, full paper and 90-second video.
- Pooled original-compact metrics cover only three folds. Never rank them
  against four-fold XGBoost, elastic-net or signed-log metrics.
- Rolling-fold bootstrap intervals use legacy development grouping and reused
  training history. They are exploratory diagnostics, not the frozen final gate.

Priority: repair evidence bindings and establish an independent evaluation path
before additional synthetic test multiplication or presentation claims.

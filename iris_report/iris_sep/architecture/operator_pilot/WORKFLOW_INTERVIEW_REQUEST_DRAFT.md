# Workflow interview request

**DRAFT — DO NOT SEND WITHOUT STUDENT/MENTOR REVIEW**

Subject: Request for a 30-minute SEP forecast workflow review

Hello,

We are student researchers studying how solar energetic particle (SEP) forecasts are evaluated and audited. Our current work does not claim a new operationally superior forecaster. Instead, we built a framework that separates three questions: repeated 24-hour occurrence, performance across distinct physical SEP episodes, and genuine warning before a new SEP onset. We are also prototyping a fail-closed validity record that exposes stale/missing inputs, provenance and `VALID / DEGRADED / ABSTAIN` decisions.

We would value a **30-minute workflow interview** to understand how an SEP alert would actually be used in your environment. We are especially interested in:

- what operational decision an alert changes;
- useful warning time;
- acceptable false-alert/review burden;
- expected behavior when a feed is delayed, stale or absent;
- provenance/audit requirements.

We are not asking for sensitive operational data and would not represent the conversation as proof of product benefit. Our first goal is simply to document one real workflow requirement and incorporate it into a versioned prospective validation protocol.

If this is appropriate, we can share a two-page research brief and a five-minute demonstration in advance.

Thank you for considering it.

— Student research team

# Live IRIS / ISEF rules verification — 2026-09-11

**Purpose:** operational compliance note for IRIS-SEP. This records only what was verified from the current official public rules pages on 2026-09-11. It does not replace the live IRIS portal, the ISEF Rules Wizard, an Adult Sponsor, or an SRC/IRB determination.

## IRIS 2026–27

Verified from the current IRIS National Fair site:

- registration/submission window: **1 August 2026 through 3 October 2026**;
- online submission requires **project synopsis + full research paper + 90-second project video**;
- IRIS offers **Physics & Astronomy (PHYS)** among its listed disciplines;
- classes 5–12 are eligible, and IRIS permits teams of exactly two students;
- the current cycle routes top performers toward Regeneron ISEF 2027.

The IRIS page contains one lower-page process line with inconsistent example dates, while the Key Dates section and FAQ both state **3 October 2026**. Treat **3 October 2026** as the current public deadline unless the authenticated submission portal or IRIS staff provide a newer controlling date.

## ISEF 2027 — core paperwork

Verified from the current Society for Science 2027 forms/rules pages:

The four forms required for all projects are:

1. **Form 1 — Checklist for Adult Sponsor / Safety Assessment**
2. **Form 1A — Student Checklist / Research Plan**
3. **Form 1B — Approval Form**
4. **Form 2A — Student Support Disclosure Form**

The Student Support Disclosure Form explicitly covers support including programming and AI use. Dates and signatures must be truthful. Do not backdate approvals.

Conditional forms remain governed by the actual methodology and research setting. For the current public-data/computational methodology, no human/animal/PHBA form is triggered by the analysis itself. Form 2C is conditional on actual work at a regulated research institution or equivalent setting; Form 7 is conditional on continuation/research progression.

## ISEF 2027 — eligibility period

Current Rules for All Projects state that the project submitted to ISEF may contain **no more than 12 months of continuous research** and may not include research performed before **January 2026**. For continuation projects, judges evaluate the current-year work and the current display/abstract must reflect the eligible current-year research.

This means the team must distinguish clearly between prior exploratory/background work and the eligible 2026–27 research contribution.

## ISEF judging weights

Current Grand Award science judging criteria assign:

- Research Question: 10 points
- Design and Methodology: 15 points
- Execution: 20 points
- Creativity & Potential Impact: 20 points
- Presentation: **35 points**
  - Poster: 10
  - Interview: **25**

Student command of the work is therefore a major competitive variable, not a cosmetic add-on.

## Category recommendation

At ISEF, **Physics and Astronomy (PHYS)** includes both:

- **Astronomy and Cosmology (AST)** — explicitly includes computational astronomy and physical properties of objects/phenomena in space;
- **Theoretical, Computational, and Quantum Physics (THE)** — computational/theoretical physics more generally.

For IRIS-SEP, **PHYS / Astronomy and Cosmology** is the strongest default because the physical question is solar/space-weather event prediction and episode structure. THE is a defensible secondary fit if the final project is framed mainly as a general computational evaluation methodology. Final category selection should follow the fair's live category list and the expertise the team wants judging the work.

## Human-participant boundary for operator interviews

If interviews with satellite operators, forecasters, aviation-radiation professionals, or other stakeholders are collected and analyzed as research data or used as competition evidence, the team must obtain the applicable **IRB/SRC determination before any participant interaction**. Current ISEF human-participant rules require review before recruitment/data collection.

Therefore:

- do **not** conduct research interviews first and seek approval later;
- treat the current operator interview materials as draft protocol only;
- ask the Adult Sponsor/SRC whether the planned workflow interview is human-participant research;
- if it is, obtain the required determination/forms/consent pathway before recruitment;
- if the conversation is purely informal product feedback and will not be used as research evidence, document that distinction and still follow the SRC's determination.

# ADR-005: Reconcile historical checklists with frozen primary scope

Accepted 2026-09-05 before locked evaluation. No scientific gate or evaluation policy changes.

The active benchmark_contract_v2.json list is authoritative: climatology,
causally available persistence, elastic-net logistic regression, XGBoost,
published SEPNET, recalibrated SEPNET-O, and IRIS primary on identical identities.
HMI image-only and AIA baselines are excluded from the first gate. Magnetic
tabular predictors are not HMI image embeddings. Auxiliary heads remain disabled.

Historical references to a required HMI-only ablation, optional image fusion,
secondary outputs, or hourly operation describe superseded scope. Preserve
those documents and failed experiments as history; do not interpret them as
current acceptance requirements. WINNER_READY_GATE comparison wording is
corrected to match the already frozen contract, not a results-driven relaxation.
The paired TSS, matched-detection FAR, calibration, lead-time and integrity
requirements in evaluation_policy_v1.json are unchanged.

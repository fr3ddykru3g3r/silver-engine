# ISEF 2027 forms and approval matrix — IRIS-SEP

**Verified against the current Society for Science 2027 forms/rules pages on 2026-09-11.**  
**Purpose:** compliance checklist only. This is not a substitute for the official forms, the IRIS/SRC determination, or student-written research-plan text.

## Immediate compliance warning

The current ISEF instructions state that the applicable forms and approvals are generally completed **before research begins** (with specified exceptions such as some post-research forms). Do not backdate or invent signatures. If the team began data collection/experimentation before obtaining the required pre-approval paperwork, disclose the real dates to the Adult Sponsor/IRIS SRC and ask for a formal eligibility determination immediately.

## Current project classification

Current described methods are computational analysis of public astronomy/space-weather data and software evaluation. The described project does not currently include:

- human participants;
- vertebrate animals;
- potentially hazardous biological agents or tissue/body fluids;
- hazardous chemicals, devices or field procedures.

Therefore no IRB, IACUC or biological/hazardous-specialist approval is indicated **from the present methodology alone**. This determination must be revisited if any method changes.

## AI support rule that directly affects this repository

Current ISEF rules allow AI as a project resource with acknowledgment, but require independent student work and student-authored presentation material. Generative AI may not write the ISEF-bound research plan, abstract, poster or citations.

Repository files labelled “drafting scaffold”, “rehearsal notes”, “review synopsis” or “AI-assisted review” must therefore be used for checking facts, logic and understanding—not pasted as final submission prose.

## Research-site question that must be answered truthfully

Before declaring Form 2C unnecessary, the students and Adult Sponsor should document:

1. Where every current-year research activity occurred (home, school, field, university/research institute, company/government site, cloud/remote environment).
2. Whether any university/research-institution scientist directly mentored or supervised the project, including virtually.
3. Whether any institution supplied restricted datasets, compute, laboratory access or equipment beyond ordinary public cloud/services.

If any answer triggers a regulated-research-institution condition, ask the SRC whether Form 2C is required. Do not infer exemption from the fact that the work is computational.

## Prospective custodian phase

If the protected prospective/custodian phase is intended to become part of the same ISEF project after the affiliated fair, its approved methodology must already be included in the research plan before competing. After an affiliated fair, additional data may be collected only under the already-approved methodology; a materially new variable or procedure cannot simply be added to the winning project.

The existing prospective phase is therefore valuable because its method is frozen now, before protected outcome access. Whether it is acceptable as part of the approved competition project is still an SRC/fair paperwork determination.

## What the team must physically do

- enter true dates, sites, team-member details and Adult Sponsor information;
- disclose AI/programming/mentor assistance on Form 2A accurately;
- have the Adult Sponsor and SRC determine whether Form 2C or any other conditional form is triggered by the real research/mentoring setting;
- do not backdate signatures;
- keep signed forms and any SRC correspondence with the project evidence archive;
- re-run the ISEF Rules Wizard before adding any new method.

## No IRB/IACUC packet drafted

No IRB or IACUC application is drafted because the current study describes no human-participant or vertebrate-animal research. Creating one would falsely imply a regulated activity that is not in the methodology. If such work is later proposed, approval must occur before that work begins.

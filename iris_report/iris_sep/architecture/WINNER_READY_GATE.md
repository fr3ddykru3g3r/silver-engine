# IRIS-SEP winner-ready gate

This is a completion checklist, not a prediction of an award. Every item must
have a hashed receipt. Scope amendment ADR-005 applies to historical checklist items.

## 1. Data and causality

- Canonical issue, observation, publication, episode, and quiet-block IDs.
- Exact feature manifest and units.
- New-crossing exclusion and already-enhanced audit.
- Chronological episode-disjoint roles with a verified 24-hour purge.
- Train-only preprocessing and feature-selection receipts.
- Independent-event counts and non-test statistical-power analysis.

## 2. Model

- Primary-only default passes generated and real-data smoke tests.
- Feature-level masks and modality-specific freshness are enforced.
- All-missing and stale-input paths abstain.
- Optional tasks have validity masks, correct conditional/censoring semantics,
  and validation-side ablations.
- Five restart-safe seeds reproduce from hashes.

## 3. Comparison

- Climatology, causally available persistence, elastic net, XGBoost, and
  reproduced SEPNET / recalibrated SEPNET-O execute on identical issue identities.
- Calibration and thresholds use their frozen validation roles only.
- Matched-detection, bootstrap, seed aggregation, and degradation tolerances
  are frozen before locked evaluation.

## 4. Locked evaluation

- Configuration, environment, cohort, partitions, features, and prediction
  interfaces are hashed first.
- Locked test is opened once.
- Paired TSS interval, matched-detection false-alarm ratio, calibration,
  warning lead time, and every leakage audit pass.
- Failure means a negative or inconclusive result, not a changed threshold.

## 5. Operator research pilot

- Versioned forecast schema, evidence hash, freshness, uncertainty, and
  abstention reason are returned on every request.
- Historical event, quiet-period, outage, stale-feed, and failure replays pass.
- Monitoring, rollback, change control, security, licensing, and incident
  documentation exist.
- External operator shadow-mode validation remains required before any
  operational-certification claim.

## 6. IRIS/ISEF evidence

- One research question and one headline result.
- Architecture, chronology, reliability, false-alarm/detection, event, quiet,
  failure, ablation, and headline-comparison visuals.
- Student notebook and ownership/assistance ledger are accurate.
- The student can derive TSS, explain every split, defend latency assumptions,
  reproduce a run, and state limitations without prompts.

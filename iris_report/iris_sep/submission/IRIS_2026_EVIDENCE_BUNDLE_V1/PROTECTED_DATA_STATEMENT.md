# Protected-data statement

The candidate prospective outcome pool begins at `2025-09-10T00:00:00Z` under the frozen prospective confirmation protocol.

During development of the historical benchmark, the two-week finish plan, the forecast-validity interface, the submission evidence bundle and the operator-pilot drafts:

- protected post-2025 labels were not queried;
- protected event identities were not inspected;
- protected onset timestamps were not inspected;
- protected model scores were not computed;
- protected running event counts were not checked for power;
- protected outcomes were not used to tune thresholds, models, missingness limits or claim wording.

Prospective predictor-side collection is a separate future phase. Genuine predictions must be written before their target windows occur, under approved execution hashes and append-only provenance. Outcome-side evaluation belongs to an independent custodian and must return only the aggregate evidence allowed by the frozen protocol.

The confirmatory information floor remains at least 50 distinct onset episodes and 500 quiet blocks. If that floor is not met, the required disposition is `INSUFFICIENT_CONFIRMATORY_INFORMATION`; the development side may not move boundaries or reveal protected identities to rescue the study.

## Model comparison does not establish superiority

The matched joint-XGBoost minus proton-free-XGBoost causal-onset TSS contrast is `-0.040 [-0.163,+0.083]`. The interval crosses zero. The project therefore does **not** claim that joint XGBoost, proton history or any particular architecture is superior on genuine onset forecasting.

## Operational alert burden is poor

For joint XGBoost under the matched new-onset evaluation:

- TP = 41
- FN = 44
- FP = 330
- TN = 6,984
- sensitivity = 48.24%
- false-alarm ratio = 88.95%
- false-positive rate = 4.51%

This is one reason the classifier is a research comparator rather than a deployable product.

## Missingness evidence has a boundary

The development-only promoted-stack missingness transfer found that causal forward-fill had the smallest probability drift at 5%, 20% and 40% random observed-cell loss. At 5–20%, paired Brier-delta intervals included zero; at 40%, Brier degradation was detectably positive. Under the `POD80_MIN_FAR` policy, 40% causal-forward-fill loss reduced TSS by about 0.2265 with a paired interval entirely below zero.

This was an already-inspected development block with only 21 positive windows. Random cell dropout is not a real sensor-outage model. It supports a `DEGRADED/ABSTAIN` concept, not a universal operational percentage threshold.

## Magnetic-map reconstruction is not validated

No physics-based magnetic-map reconstruction arm was validated in the promoted missingness transfer. It remains experimental and earns no prospective role unless a separately preregistered hidden real-map comparison beats persistence and preserves downstream causal-onset utility.

## Historical causality is incomplete

The 259-variable historical feature set does not have a publication-time receipt for every variable. The historical replay also lacks a programmed purge at every role boundary. These limitations prevent an operational-readiness or untouched-validation claim.

## Protected prospective evidence is intentionally absent

The prospective protocol is frozen but unexecuted. The development side has not opened protected post-2025 labels to check power or improve the result. If future independent custody fails the information floor or produces a null result, that result must be accepted rather than redesigned on the same cohort.
